# Google Play Product Details (English)

## Short Description
Sound meter: live dB, min/avg/peak, history; optional color themes

## Full Description

dB Meter Caju is a sound meter and decibel meter (noise / SPL meter) for measuring ambient noise in dB.

Check surrounding noise in real time, with live min, average, peak, percentiles and session history.

Real-time measurement
• One tap shows the current ambient sound level in dB
• Audio sampling that reflects sound intensity
• Color-coded noise level indicators

Statistics
• Live minimum, maximum and average
• Percentiles (P50, P90, P95) for noise distribution
• Peak recording for the loudest moments

Look & themes (optional)
• Paper-style dial; pick from color themes and paper texture
• Light, dark, or system appearance; several font choices
• Settings also include app info and brand links

Languages: English, Chinese, Spanish, Hindi, Arabic, Portuguese, Bengali, Russian, Japanese, German

Where it helps
• Home: household noise
• Work: workplace noise
• Study: quieter study space
• Public places: surrounding noise
• Hearing awareness: long-term noise exposure

Privacy
The microphone is used only while you measure. Audio is not collected in the background. Measurement data stays on your device and is not uploaded to a server.
